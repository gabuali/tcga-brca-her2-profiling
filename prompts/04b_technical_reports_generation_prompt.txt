### Section 2: Clinical HER2 Status Derivation & Discordance Adjudication
- Script: `src/adjudicate_her2.py`
- Inputs: `data/brca_tcga_clinical_data.csv` (N = 1,108).
- Methodology: 2-step hierarchical cascade:
  * Step A: Discrete numerical IHC score (0, 1, 2, 3) precedence over qualitative text labels.
  * Step B: Reflex FISH adjudication for equivocal (2+) or missing IHC.
  * Discordance audit: Identifying and flagging conflicts between IHC and FISH.
- Results & Tables:
  * Master Contingency Table: Negative (786, 70.9%), Positive (172, 15.5%), Indeterminate (150, 13.5%).
  * Discordance Summary Table: 42 flagged discordant cases broken down by pattern (e.g., IHC 1+ but FISH Pos, IHC 3+ but FISH Neg).
  * Data Completeness Table: Breakdown by IHC and FISH availability.
- Outputs Referenced: `results/her2_clinical_status.csv`, `results/brca_her2_status_adjudicated.tsv`, `results/her2_adjudication_summary.xlsx` (Sheets: `1_Contingency_Table`, `2_Discordant_Summary_and_Rows`, `3_Data_Completeness_Breakdown`).

### Section 3: RNA-Seq Diagnostic Profiling & Gate C Routing
- Script: `src/normalize_rnaseq.R`
- Inputs: `data/tcga.brca.rsem.csv` (1,212 samples x 20,502 genes).
- Methodology & Diagnostic Gates:
  * Gate A: Max matrix value = 2,066,039.16 (linear count space).
  * Gate B: Sample column sums = 16.1M to 36.3M (unnormalized library sizes).
  * Gate C: 75th percentile of non-zero genes scaled uniformly to ~1,000 (999.17 to 1,003.09, CV = 0.0456%), confirming TCGA Level 3 Upper-Quartile normalized RSEM.
- Decision Point: Rationale for routing to `edgeR` (TMM scale factors) + `limma-voom` variance stabilization rather than `DESeq2`.
- Prevalence Filter: Retaining counts >= 15 in >= 200 samples preserves 15,632 / 20,502 genes (76.2%), retaining ERBB2 and all 17q12 amplicon genes.

### Section 4: PCA Outlier Screening, Outlier Removal & Cohort Re-Normalization
- Script: `src/normalize_rnaseq.R`
- Methodology: Principal Component Analysis with unit-variance scaling (`scale. = TRUE`).
  * Critical Decision Point: Explaining why unit-variance scaling was essential to prevent hyper-variable lineage genes like `ESR1` (which correlates r = 0.80 with unscaled PC2) from falsely flagging 145 ER-negative/Basal-like tumors as outliers.
- Outlier Identification: Robust MAD threshold (`|Z_PC1| >= 3` or `|Z_PC2| >= 3`) flagged 19 extreme technical outliers (18 Primary Tumors, 1 Adjacent Normal).
- Embedded Figures:
  * Figure 1A: `pca_before_outlier_removal.png` (PCA showing 19 extreme outliers in red).
  * Figure 1B: `pca_after_outlier_removal.png` (PCA showing clean, balanced 1,193-sample distribution).
- Re-normalization Results: Clean 1,193-sample cohort (1,075 Primary Tumor, 111 Adjacent Normal, 7 Metastasis).
- Outputs Referenced: `results/pca_outliers_removed.csv`, `results/rsem_sample_metadata.csv`, `results/matrix_normalized_linear.csv`, `results/matrix_normalized_log2_stabilized.csv`.

### Section 8: Multimodal Purity- and Amplicon-Aware Machine Learning Modeling (Step 6 & Plot 7)

- Sub-Section: Why Probability Threshold Shifting is Required (The Problem):
  * Mathematical explanation: Severe minority penalty (4.56x) inflates output probabilities near decision boundary.
  * Result of naive 0.50 cutoff: Excess false positives (60 in LR, 6 in RF).
  * The solution: Out-of-fold Youden's J optimization re-calibrates the decision threshold to empirical optimum (0.6240 for LR, 0.5880 for RF).
- Full OOF Comparative Metrics Table (Default 0.50 vs. Shifted Youden for LR and RF).

  * Highlight: Amplicon_Z + Purity account for 49.11% of model decision weight.
